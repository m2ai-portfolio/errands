## Recording checklist

- Record the live run first, then cut the voiceover to it. Keep the real call audio for at least one exchange per call.
- Show the words "Strands Agents" on screen at least twice (title card and architecture).
- Upload to YouTube as Public or Unlisted-with-link (the rules reject private videos). Check the length is under 5:00.
